🛡️ Cyber Awareness

Learn how to stay safe online

📱 QR Code Safety

  • Do not scan QR codes from unknown sources.
  • Always check the website address before opening it.
  • Never enter OTP, PIN or password on an unknown website.
  • Be careful with QR codes asking for urgent payments.

⚠️ Common Online Scams

  • Fake shopping websites
  • Phishing links and messages
  • Fake prize or lottery messages
  • Fake job offers
  • Fake banking or KYC messages

🔐 Strong Password

  • Use a strong and unique password.
  • Do not share your password with anyone.
  • Avoid using your name or birthday as a password.
  • Use two-factor authentication whenever possible.

🔑 OTP Safety

  • Never share your OTP with anyone.
  • Bank employees will not ask for your OTP.
  • Do not enter OTP on suspicious websites.

  • Keep your phone and apps updated.

  • Do not click unknown links.
  • Check website addresses carefully.
  • Report suspicious activity instead of ignoring it.
