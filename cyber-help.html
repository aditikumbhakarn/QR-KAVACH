🚨 Cyber Crime Help

Take quick action if you face an online scam or cyber crime.

⚠️ If You Are Scammed

  1. Stay calm and do not send more money.
  2. Take screenshots of messages, QR codes, links and transactions.
  3. Contact your bank or payment app immediately if money was transferred.
  4. Change your passwords if your account details were shared.
  5. Report the incident to the official cyber crime portal.

💰 If Money Was Lost

If money has been transferred because of a cyber fraud, report it as quickly as possible.

Use the official Government of India cyber crime reporting portal to report online financial fraud and other cyber crimes.

Report Cyber Crime

🛡️ Protect Yourself

  • Never share your OTP with anyone.
  • Never share your ATM PIN or password.
  • Check links carefully before opening them.
  • Do not scan unknown QR codes.
  • Use strong and unique passwords.
  • Enable two-factor authentication whenever possible.
